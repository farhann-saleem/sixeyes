Redirecting “Create” to Gemini fails on the mechanics:

- We cannot auto-fill Gemini’s prompt, auto-select **Create image**, or attach the user’s photo. That is another site’s UI. A URL cannot drive those controls.

- Gemini image mode does not make LTX aesthetic videos. The video credit problem is untouched.
